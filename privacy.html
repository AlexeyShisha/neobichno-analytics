Privacy Policy

Neobichno Analytics uses TikTok Login Kit to connect a user's TikTok account after explicit authorization.

The service may receive basic profile information, account statistics, and public video information according to the scopes granted by the user. This data is used to provide analytics and track content performance over time.

Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely on the server and are not displayed in the public analytics interface.

Neobichno Analytics does not publish, modify, or delete TikTok content.

Users may revoke access through their TikTok account settings.
